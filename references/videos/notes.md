# Reference video notes (learn techniques, never copy)

| | agentstick.mp4 (~73s) | singeexplique.mp4 (~64s) |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | Split screen: top "stage" (maps, icons, mini scenes), bottom 2 characters talking | One clear subject on white |
| Text | One bold word at center, synced to speech | Handwritten words appear alone with sparkles |
| Pace | Shot change every 2–4s; something changes ~every 1s (pose, icon, card) | Same; pop-ins ~every 1s |
| Retention | "???" mystery cards revealed one by one; detailed color scene at key reveal; iceberg zoom-out ending; "FOLLOW!" wave | Running counter ("🍌 +5"); recurring worker vs rich characters; visual metaphors (carrot on stick); ending returns to opening scene |

Avoid: real politicians' faces as-is (agentstick does this), long in-image text.

## Deep pass (4 fps sampling + audio levels)
- agentstick: hard cuts only (no fancy transitions); "jump zooms" between wide two-shot and close-up; narrator arm/pose changes every 0.25–0.5s (drawn pose frames); caption = every spoken word, one at a time; motion blur when characters enter; mystery cards pop then reveal one (others greyed). Continuous music bed (~-28 dB floor, never silent) under voice.
- singeexplique: hard cuts, holds 1–2s, handwritten word reveals with sparkles; voice-only, real silences (5% of windows < -45 dB).
- Takeaway for Benny: word-by-word keyword captions, pose/zoom change every ~0.5–1s, motion blur on fast moves, quiet music bed optional, hard cuts + purposeful zoom/slide rather than flashy presets.

## virtuosonetwork "Poor vs Rich" (5 screenshots, Oct 2026)
- Look: cream graph-paper notebook background; watercolor/pencil characters; flat muted fills (red, green, blue, tan) with pencil hatching.
- Motion vocabulary: handwritten text writes on with a pencil following the stroke; highlighter swipes behind key words (red POOR, green RICH); underline draw-on; "blueprint" sketch boxes whose construction lines overshoot corners, then the fill wipes in; calendar card that flips (SAVING 2023 → A YEAR LATER AUG); loan letter where the pencil writes the amount, then APPROVED stamp slams; numbered step headers (④ MOVE OUT...); stacked bar chart drawn and filled segment by segment; characters walk across frame.
