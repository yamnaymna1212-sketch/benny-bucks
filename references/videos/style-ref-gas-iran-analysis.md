# Style reference: style-ref-gas-iran-60s.mp4 (67.5s, 576x1024, 30fps)
- Background: plain white with a faint light-gray grid (graph paper). No color fields, no keylines, no shadows.
- Hook (0–6s): split frame, no cut. "WHY" in black on a yellow highlighter banner at the top; US flag + "$4.32 PER GALLON" tag + blue pump on the left, Iran flag + "$0.03" + green pump on the right, Benny in the middle (thinking, "??" marks). Red hand-drawn arrows point at each flag. Slow continuous pan left→right (~5s) from the US side to the Iran side, following the narration.
- Pace: ~62 visual changes in 67s ≈ 1 new shot every ~1.1s (vs ~4.5s per line in our collage videos). Most lines get 2–3 shots.
- Benny: animated (moving) line-art cutouts on white, often 2 Benny poses side by side; facial reactions change every shot.
- Captions: ONE word at a time, yellow fill + thick black outline, small, placed just above/next to the subject (upper third), not in a fixed bottom band.
- Emphasis text: big colored keywords ("$208" green, "cheaper" red italic, "x100", "$5", "15,000 rial"), hand-drawn arrows (orange/red), "=" signs between object and number.
- Objects: clean flat illustrations + some real photos (penny, oil barrel); one real person (Fed chair) — we avoid real people (use cast-fed-guy).
- Audio: loud master (mean −11 dB); voice + SFX.
