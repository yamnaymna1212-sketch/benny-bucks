## What it is
A toy-diorama TOWN seen as a cutaway: a house with the ground-floor front wall removed (living room visible, upstairs closed), set on a street. One continuous world, camera flies between props; every spoken idea gets a physical prop.
## Look
- Saturated flat colours + soft PBR, bright blue sky with thin clouds, striped mowed grass, brick/tile/pavement textures, road with double yellow line. Clean low-poly, slight bloom. Not photoreal.
- Money = red-orange GBP 50 bundles (readable at any size), always physical (on sofa, truck bed, conveyor).
