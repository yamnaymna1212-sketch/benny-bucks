# Animation system

## Method: cutout ("paper puppet")
Characters are split into layered parts (head, hair, eyes, brows, mouth, body, arms, legs). Animate by swapping parts (expressions, mouth shapes), rotating parts (arms/head), and moving the whole rig. Reuse the same parts in every video.

## Always-on life
- Blink every 3–5s.
- Mouth flaps / simple mouth shapes synced to voiceover when speaking.
- Light idle breathing/bob; optional subtle line wobble (hand-drawn boil).

## Motion library
pop-in (scale with small overshoot), slide-in, grow/shrink, count-up numbers, bounce (joy), shake (shock/anger), fly-away (money leaving), swap (object changes state). Use easing; keep moves short (0.2–0.6s).

## Camera
- Subtle push-in (≈3–8% over a scene) for tension or emphasis.
- Pull-out for big-picture reveals.
- Small pans to follow action. No fast spinning or heavy shake.

## Transitions
Default hard cut. Also: white flash (surprise), slide wipe (time/place change), zoom-through an object into the next scene (e.g. into a coin, a door, a phone screen). Each transition needs a story reason.

## Scene continuity
- Start each scene from where the last one ended (same object, same direction of motion, or same character).
- Carry a "linking object" across scenes (the coin, the card, the house).
- Keep screen direction consistent (Benny left / Mia right in contrasts).
- Time jumps are shown on screen (calendar flip, clock, "10 years later").
