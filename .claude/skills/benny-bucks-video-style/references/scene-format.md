# Scene format

## Frame
- 1080×1920, vertical 9:16, 30 fps.
- Safe zone: keep key text/faces out of top ~250px and bottom ~400px, and right ~150px (TikTok UI).
- Large, bold, short on-screen text (1–5 words), key numbers big.

## Length
45–75s total; ~10–14 scenes of 3–7s.

## Story structure
1. Hook (0–3s): surprising claim + strong image.
2. Setup (3–12s): Benny's situation.
3. Story (12–40s): step by step consequences.
4. Contrast (40–55s): the other path (often Mia).
5. Payoff (55–65s): clear lesson + real numbers.
6. Ending (last ~3s): Benny reaction + follow prompt.

## Writing
Plain American English, short sentences, one idea per scene. Not financial advice: explain, don't promise returns; state assumptions (e.g. "at 7% average annual return").
