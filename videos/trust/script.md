Hook (kept): "Why don't billionaires ever leave their money directly to their children?"
Hook visual: LEFT "−$394M ESTATE TAX" (leave $1B directly) vs RIGHT "$0 TAX ON THE GROWTH" (trust). Benny points left, then flips to point right.

Fact fixes vs the pasted script:
- Estate tax 40% applies only ABOVE the exemption: $15M per person in 2026 (OBBBA, indexed). $1B estate → 40% × ($1B − $15M) ≈ $394M ("almost $400 million").
- Moving billions into an irrevocable trust is a GIFT: above the exemption it pays the same 40% gift tax. The real trick: move assets in EARLY,
  while they're worth ~$15M (inside the exemption); all later growth happens inside the trust, outside Rick's estate (no estate tax on it).
- US has no federal "inheritance tax" paid by the son; the estate tax is paid by the estate.
- Lawsuits/divorce: spendthrift trusts USUALLY keep a beneficiary's creditors and ex-spouse from the trust assets (state rules vary) — not "completely bulletproof".
- Dynasty trusts can last for generations in some states.
