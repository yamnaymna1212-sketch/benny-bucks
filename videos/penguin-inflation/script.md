# EP2 "Inflation explained with fish" — v2 (formula version, ~62 s)

1. Last year, Doug's snow cone cost one fish. Today it costs two. Doug did nothing.
2. This is Doug. Doug has five fish.
3. Doug wants a snow cone.
4. But one day, the Big Seal turns on the fish machine.
5. Brrrrrr.
6. Now there are fish everywhere.
7. Everybody is rich!
8. And everybody wants a snow cone.
9. Puffin has ten snow cones. And one hundred penguins.
10. Puffin thinks.
11. Two fish now.
12. This is called inflation.
13. Doug.
14. More fish. Same snow cones. So every fish buys less.
15. Even Doug's fish under the bed buy less. They never left the bed.
16. In real life, prices went up about twenty-three percent since 2020.
17. Doug finally gets his snow cone.
18. It's tiny.
19. (Puffin, on screen: "is same snow cone.")
20. That's called shrinkflation. Next episode.
21. What got more expensive for you? Tell me in the comments.

Fact: US CPI-U Jan 2020 257.971 → Jan 2025 317.671 (BLS) = +23%.
