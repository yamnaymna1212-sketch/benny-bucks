# Channel foundation — "Doug's Price Gap" (locked 2026-10-28)

Read this before every new episode, together with script-formula.md.

## Why this exists (analytics, Sep 29 – Oct 26)
- 415K views, 218,923 followers, net followers -424. Traffic: For You 90.3%, Search 8.3% -> the existing followers almost never see the videos.
- Viewers: 100% new every week, 83.6% US, 18-34 = 77%. Followers skew older (25-44), so the old follower base does not match the current audience.
- Best new-follower count on any single video: 28. Follow rate is the leak, not hook or views.
- Winners = price gap USA vs another country (gas $4.47 vs $0.13, $4.41 vs $0.11, cash vs card $15). Losers (300-1K views) = explainers, credit score, rent, Tesla vs Toyota, abstract rich-vs-poor.

## The one niche (one sentence TikTok and the viewer must both get)
"Doug shows the price gap: what Americans pay vs what the rest of the world pays."

Every episode = ONE thing, ONE American price, ONE other-place price, a "Why".
80% of posts in this lane. Max 20% experiments. No more "Explained by Doug", no abstract rich/poor, no credit-score/tax/laundering topics.

## Fixed series packaging
- Series name: **Doug's Price Gap #N** (number every episode, on the cover corner, small).
- Cover: same layout always — flag + big price left, flag + big price right, "Why?" on top, same font, same background colour family.
- Hook (0-3 s): "Why" + both prices visible in the first frame. "ting" at 0 s.
- Order: shock -> reason -> confirming number -> ending.
- Ending (last line, every time): promise the NEXT price gap by name + follow reason.
  e.g. "Tomorrow: why a coffee is $6 here and $1 in Vietnam. Follow so you don't miss it."
- Doug identity: the poor penguin who pays full price. He is the victim in the American side, never the explainer.

## Caption + hashtags (search is 8.3% of traffic — make it count)
- Line 1 = the exact question people would type: "Why is gas $4.47 in America but $0.13 in Venezuela?"
- Then "Doug's Price Gap #N".
- Fixed tags only: #gasprices #pricecomparison #moneytok (+ 1 topic tag). Never off-topic tags (no #teslacar etc.).

## Profile
- Bio: "Why Americans pay more than the world. New price gap daily."
- Pin 3: gas USA vs Venezuela, gas USA vs Iran, cash vs card.
- Make an "Doug's Price Gap" playlist and add every episode.
- Optional, reversible: set off-niche videos under ~1K views to Private (do not delete).

## Cadence + proof
- 1 episode/day, same time, 21 days in a row. Judge after 21 days, not before.
- Part 2/3 of anything that passes ~5K views (gas especially).
- Reply to comments with a video; pin a comment asking "which country next?".
- KPI to read weekly (not views): new followers per 1K views (target >= 2), net followers > 0, followers share of traffic rising above ~1%.
