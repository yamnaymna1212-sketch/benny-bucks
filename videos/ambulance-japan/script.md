## Facts (sources)
- $1,277 = average emergency ALS ground-ambulance charge 2020, excl. mileage (FAIR Health white paper 2022).
- Avg allowed (in-network) ALS amount $758 (2020); California avg allowed emergency ALS $1,461 (2022) — FAIR Health.
- No Surprises Act excludes ground ambulances (Peterson-KFF; FAIR Health).
- Japan: ambulance transport free (municipal fire departments). Matsusaka (Mie) charges ¥7,700 since 2024-06-01 if not admitted, at 3 core hospitals; dispatches −10.2% (kuruma-news, financial-field).
- Uber: ≥7% drop in per-capita ambulance rate after UberX entry, 766 cities (Moskatel & Slusky working paper; CNBC 2017).
