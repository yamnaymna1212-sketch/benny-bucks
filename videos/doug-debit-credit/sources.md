# EP6 Debit vs Credit — sources / truth notes
- Avg credit card interest ~22% (Fed G.19, accounts assessed interest: 22.15% Q2 2026, ~22.36% Aug 2026): https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/TERMCBCCINTNS
- $50 carried 1 year at ~22% APR (monthly compounding) = ~$11-12 interest -> "more than ten dollars" is true.
- Paying the full statement balance by the due date = no interest on purchases (grace period; CARD Act: at least 21 days from statement to due date).
- On-time payments can help a credit score ("can go up", not "will").
- Debit: money leaves the account immediately (on settlement), no interest.
- Hook: "What the difference" (user wording) split DEBIT $50 | CREDIT $50, same amount both sides; Hook camera rule (zoom on "debit", then "credit").
