Rules we follow (18 U.S.C. 504 / 31 CFR 411, not legal advice): educational/newsworthy use; colour images are ONE-SIDED (backs = blank paper, never the printed back);
size > 150 % of a real note (money-kit builds everything at 1.51 x 156 x 66.3 mm and throws if scale <= 1.5). Keep the SPECIMEN overprint. Never print the textures.
Strap colours: American Bankers Association standard ($100 red, $2,000 violet, $10,000 mustard).
