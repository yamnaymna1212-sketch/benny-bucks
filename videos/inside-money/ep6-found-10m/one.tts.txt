He finds ten million dollars under his house, and he's so happy he does something he'll regret for life. His name is Danny, and he wakes up two friends. First friend, Rick, screams, take it to the bank and a SWAT team and a helicopter will land on your lawn! Second friend, Tara, whispers, keep it. We'll buy a mansion, a Ferrari, and fly first class forever. She's already picking the color of the Ferrari. Danny listens to both. Then he thinks for exactly one second. He loads the trunk into his old pickup and drives straight to the bank. He drags it inside. A hundred kilos of hundred dollar bills. No helicopter. No SWAT. The teller just smiles. For any cash over ten thousand dollars, the bank files one routine form. The manager hands him a certificate, takes photos, and buys him a coffee. Danny walks home proud. Then he checks the mailbox. A letter from the IRS. Found treasure counts as income. A couple found four thousand dollars inside an old piano, and the court said, pay tax. Ten million dollars of income lands in the top bracket. Federal tax, about three point six five million dollars. And that's before state tax. Danny keeps six point three five million. Still rich. Still legal. His only regret? Telling Tara.
