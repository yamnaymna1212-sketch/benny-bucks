# EP6 facts (checked 2026-11-01; all web, secondary sources unless noted - re-check on render day)
- Treasure trove = gross income in the year it is reduced to undisputed possession (Treas. Reg. 1.61-14). Case: Cesarini v. United States (N.D. Ohio 1969, aff'd 6th Cir. 1970, 428 F.2d 812): couple bought a used piano ~1957 for ~$15, found $4,467 cash in 1964, taxed as income. Sources: Wikipedia page, Tax Notes, Foster Garvey PDF. VERIFY primary opinion on CourtListener.
- Real $10M story: Saddle Ridge Hoard (California couple, 2013, 1,427 gold coins, ~$10M). Coverage 2014: tax estimated ~39.6% federal + 13.3% CA; California has no treasure trove law. Final tax paid NOT confirmed. Use as a 'real case' mention only if sourced.
- Cash over $10,000 in one business day -> bank files a Currency Transaction Report (FinCEN, 31 CFR 1010.311). Routine, legal, invisible. Structuring (splitting deposits to dodge it) is a federal crime (31 USC 5324). Form number 112 (older 104) and 15-day deadline: verify on fincen.gov. NEVER mention structuring on screen as a tip.
