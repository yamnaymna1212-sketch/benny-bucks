- $1 note: 2.61 x 6.14 in, ~0.0043 in thick, ~1 gram (BEP / uscurrency.gov) -> 100 notes ~ 10.9 mm (money-kit NT). $1,000,000 in $1 bills = 1,000,000 notes ~ 1 metric ton. RE-CHECK on bep.gov before the full script.
- Strap colours (ABA standard): 100 x $1 = $100 strap = BLUE (Wikipedia "Currency strap" table: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currency_strap). money-kit.js fixed 2026-11-10 (it had red).
- Scene money: 12 bricks x (8 x 4 x 26) = 9,984 straps x $100 = $998,400 ~ "$1,000,000" (illustrative). Notes drawn at 151 % size, one-sided (18 U.S.C. 504, money-kit.js).
- IRS + satellites: NO evidence found that the IRS uses satellite/aerial imagery for income tax (search 2026-11-10). Aerial imagery IS used by county property-tax assessors (Hawaii County Pictometry: https://hawaiitribune-herald.com/2013/04/09/hawaii-news/aerial-photos-help-fight-tax-scofflaws/ ; Greene County PA: https://greenecountypa.gov/News-Pictometry-Investment-Assists-Tax-Assessors) and abroad (France AI found 20,356 undeclared pools, ~EUR 5 M recovered in one departement: https://petapixel.com/2022/08/30/french-officials-use-satellite-photos-and-ai-to-spot-unregistered-pools , https://www.thelocal.fr/20250228/french-tax-authorities-seize-e5-million-for-undeclared-swimming-pools).
- IRS methods (record-based): information-return matching (Automated Underreporter), audits, indirect methods (expenditures vs reported income), whistleblower awards up to 30 % (VERIFY on irs.gov before the script).
- Holding cash is not itself taxed; the income it came from is (VERIFY on irs.gov before the script). Cash deposits over $10,000 -> bank files a Currency Transaction Report (FinCEN; already used in EP6).

## FULL SCRIPT facts (verified 2026-11-10)
- "IRS pays rewards for tips": IRS Whistleblower Office, Form 211, awards under IRC 7623 (https://www.irs.gov/compliance/whistleblower-informant-award). No % said on screen (15-30 % applies only to cases over $2M).
- "Keeping cash at home is legal": no US law limits cash kept at home; tax is due on the INCOME (IRC 61). Large cash bank transactions > $10,000 are reported (FinCEN CTR).
- "Tax evasion: up to five years in prison": 26 U.S.C. 7201 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/7201).
- "The fraud penalty alone is seventy-five percent": 26 U.S.C. 6663(a) civil fraud penalty = 75 % of the underpayment due to fraud (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6663).
- "They don't need a satellite": no evidence the IRS uses satellite photos; the satellite room in the hook is a what-if dramatization. Form 1040 shown = generic layout with a blank income amount.
