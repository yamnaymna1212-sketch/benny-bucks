# EP17 script v2 (2026-11-07). HYPOTHETICAL what-if. User's hook (grammar fixed only). The ANSWER is held back until ~85% of the video (retention): hook -> open loop 'three things decide it' -> three CLUES -> answer -> vote.
Voice text = vo.tts.txt (~231 words; EP15 ran 3.78 words/s => ~61 s at Kokoro am_liam 1.0, max_gap .08, ONE take). MEASURE the real take; need 61-65 s. If < 61 s add a short line ("Mike checks his pay twice." is already in), never slow the voice. If > 65 s cut the critics line.
RULE: nothing before 'So, the answer.' may say "nothing changes / same tax". Clue 1 only says WHO writes the law (a clue, not the answer).

1. What happens to your tax if El-Sayed becomes president today?
2. Three things decide it, and the last one decides everything.   (open loop)
3. He just won a Senate seat in Michigan, and Trump is president today. But picture it.
4. Meet Mike. Every Friday, a slice of his pay goes to taxes before he sees a cent.
5. Now it's President El-Sayed's first morning, and Mike checks his pay twice.
6. CLUE ONE. Who writes the tax law? Not the president. Congress does. That's Article One of the Constitution.
7. CLUE TWO. What does El-Sayed want? Higher taxes on billionaires, a wealth tax.
8. His campaign's calculator tests five percent on the wealth of about nine hundred thirty-eight billionaires.
9. That's a tax on what they own, not on what they earn.
10. Supporters say the richest should pay more. Critics say money can move or hide, and collections could fall short.
11. CLUE THREE. What about Mike? El-Sayed says he hopes to avoid raising taxes on families near one hundred thousand dollars. Experts disagree.
12. Stay with me, the answer is next. So, the answer.   (REVEAL, ~52 s, after a short whoosh)
13. Tomorrow morning, Mike's pay stub is the same. Not one cent changed.
14. A president can ask, and he can sign or veto. But a tax only changes after Congress passes a bill, and beating a veto takes two thirds.
15. Nobody can promise that vote.
16. So, Mike's tax after that vote: up or down? Comment UP or DOWN.

## Retention design
- Line 2 opens the loop; clue cards 1/2/3 are visible counters (big 1 2 3, numbers only, like EP16 hook) so the viewer feels progress toward 'the answer'.
- Line 12 'So, the answer.' = camera lands on the pay stub, ting, then the reveal (stub unchanged, big 0 cents glow).
- Ending = vote question about what the vote will do (neutral, no promise).
