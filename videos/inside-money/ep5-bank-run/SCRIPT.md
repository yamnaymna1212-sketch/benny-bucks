15. So the money isn't missing. It's out working in the economy.                                   [bills circulate: house, shop, back to bank]
16. Banks run on trust. Most people just don't all ask at once.                                    [calm lobby, Mike nods]
17. Did you think your cash was sitting in a vault? Yes or no?                                     [question card]
