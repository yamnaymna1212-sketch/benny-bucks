# EP1 "Where does your bank keep everyone's money?" — facts + credits
- Reserve requirement 0% since Mar 26, 2020: https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/reservereq.htm ; unchanged for 2026: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-11-24/html/2025-20744.htm
- Banks hold reserves mostly as balances in accounts at the Federal Reserve; banks order/deposit currency through Federal Reserve Banks (armored carriers): https://www.federalreserve.gov/paymentsystems/coin_currcircmechanism.htm
- FDIC insures deposits up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category: https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance
- Simplification: "the bank lends it out" (lending is limited by capital and liquidity rules, not only deposits).
