# Inside Money — script + edit structure (v1, 2026-10-29)
Built from: Zack D. Films hook pattern ("most people assume..."), the You Built It pool video we measured (no cuts, camera rhythm, 1-word captions),
our own TikTok data (Iran gas 27% full-watch vs penguin 5-8%), and well-known attention psychology. Audience: US, 15-35.

## The 7 beats (60-65 s version; the 15 s test uses beats 1, 2, 4, 6, 7)
| # | Time | Beat | What it does | Psychology |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-2 s | **Misconception hook** ("Most people think..." / "Why...?") + the object on screen, already moving | States what the viewer believes | *Pattern interrupt*: the brain stops scrolling when a belief it holds is about to be challenged |
| 2 | 2-4 s | **Twist**: "It doesn't." / the shocking real number | Breaks the belief in 1 short line | *Prediction error*: being wrong releases attention; a gap opens (*curiosity gap*, Loewenstein) |
| 3 | 4-6 s | **Open loop**: "So where does it go?" | Promises an answer later | *Zeigarnik effect*: unfinished questions are remembered and pull the viewer forward |
| 4 | 6-40 s | **Chain, not list**: each line = "and then / but / so" | Follow ONE object (your $1,000) step by step | *Causal chains* are easy to follow and impossible to stop mid-way; "3 reasons" lists let people predict the end and leave |
| 5 | every 6-8 s | **Re-hook**: a new number, a "wait", a reveal | Resets attention | Attention decays in seconds; a *new stimulus* (orienting response) restarts it |
| 6 | 40-55 s | **Payoff**: the answer to the open loop + 1 real sourced number | Closes the loop | *Closure* feels rewarding -> likes / saves |
| 7 | last 3 s | **Loop line + comment question** | Last sentence leads back into the first frame | *Rewatch loop* (counts as watch time) + a binary question gets comments |

## Script rules (simple and clear for 15-35)
- 8-12 words per line, 1 idea per line, 1 line = 1 shot. Words a 12-year-old knows. Speak to "you".
- Follow ONE concrete object (a $1,000 bundle), never abstract nouns. Show it, then name it.
- Numbers: only real, sourced (facts.md), max 2-3 per video, said slowly and shown big. No promises, no "do this", no exaggeration (script safety rule).
- Every line must push the chain forward; if a line can be removed without breaking the chain, remove it.

## Edit rules (why each one keeps people watching)
- **Never a still frame.** Camera always moving on one spline (no cuts): slow open 0-3 s, dive/pull-out every 6-9 s, slow settle at the end (measured on the reference). Stillness = exit point.
- **Something new every 2-3 s** (object moves, number appears, light changes).
- **Captions**: 1-2 words, Montserrat ExtraBold, white, thin dark stroke + soft shadow, pop-in 80 ms. **Red** = price / loss / danger, **green** = money gained / safe, white = rest. Colour only key words (*isolation effect*: the coloured word is the one remembered).
- **Sound**: no silence ever (room/air bed under the voice), whoosh on every camera move, soft riser 0.5 s before a twist, low boom on the twist, clean ding on the key number, cash/paper sounds on money. Sound tells the brain "something changed" before the eye sees it.
- **Voice**: 160-180 words/min, gaps under 0.08 s, energy rises toward the payoff.
