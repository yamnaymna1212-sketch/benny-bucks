## Script (~200 words, target 61-65 s, one continuous take, slow clear voice)
1. If you dig a hole in the forest to hide from a SWAT team, you have a hidey hole.
2. But the first rainstorm fills it with water, so you dig sideways into the hill and slope the floor up, so the rain runs right back out.
3. This is your tunnel.
4. But SWAT finds your hole and drops a flashbang down it, so you make every tunnel zigzag.
5. And the flash hits the dirt wall instead of you.
6. But then they pump tear gas into the entrance, so you dip the tunnel under a pool of water, and the gas can't get through.
7. This is your water trap.
8. But then they crawl in with flashlights, so you dig a fake tunnel that goes in a circle, and they crawl right back outside.
9. This is your maze.
10. But their dogs sniff out your air holes, so you rub every hole with pine sap, and the dogs smell nothing but forest.
11. But sooner or later, they'll find every entrance. So you dig one more at the bottom of the river.
12. And that entrance is not underwater by mistake. They search every inch of the forest, but nobody searches the bottom of a river.
13. Add a hammock, a lantern, and a raccoon that keeps stealing your snacks.
14. Congratulations! You built a secret base.

## TTS string (one take)
Join lines 1-14 with spaces. If the take is > 65 s: cut line 5 first, then shorten line 12 ("Nobody searches the bottom of a river."). Never speed up.
