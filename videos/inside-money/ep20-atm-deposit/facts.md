# EP20 facts — ATM cash deposit (checked 2026-11-11). Every number on screen / in the voice needs a line here.

- Cash at the bank's OWN (proprietary) ATM: must be available by the start of business on the 2nd business day after the banking day of deposit (Reg CC 12 CFR 229.10 + official interpretation; e.g. deposited Monday -> Wednesday). https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/229.10 ; https://bankersonline.com/qa/reg-cc-depositary-bank-atm-deposits . Many banks release sooner -> script says "CAN hold ... up to two business days".
- Cash or checks at ANOTHER bank's (nonproprietary) ATM: available not later than the 5th business day (12 CFR 229.12(f)). https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-II/subchapter-A/part-229/subpart-B/section-229.12 -> "up to five".
- Cutoff: deposits after the cutoff hour (ATMs may use a cutoff as early as 12 noon) or on a non-banking day count on the NEXT banking day (12 CFR 229.19(a)(5)). https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/229.19 ; https://bankcustomer.gov/get-answers/bank-accounts/funds-availability/faq-banking-funds-available-11.html -> Friday night = Monday -> available Wednesday (worst case).
- Deposit ATMs validate each note with optical / magnetic-ink / UV / size sensors and reject counterfeit or damaged notes (USPTO patent 9336638 "Media item validation"; https://www.atmmarketplace.com/blogs/can-atms-detect-counterfeit-bills/). "Counts every bill in one second" = illustrative speed (no number on screen).
- Banks send excess cash to the Federal Reserve; Reserve Banks count it, check authenticity/fitness and shred unfit notes (~15% of deposited notes destroyed) (Boston Fed https://www.bostonfed.org/news-and-events/news/2019/07/when-bills-go-bad.aspx ; https://frbservices.org/financial-services/cash/currency.html).
- Armored-truck pickup timing varies by bank -> script says "Later", no number.
- ATM errors (miscount): consumers can dispute electronic fund transfer errors under Reg E error resolution (12 CFR 1005.11); the receipt is evidence. Script: "keep the receipt" (general, no promise).
