# FORMULA "Crime vs Legal" (from EP20 hidden cash, user: works on TikTok, 2026-11-14)
Reuse this structure + style for new videos. Time stamps = EP20 (61.4 s, ~250 words, Kokoro am_liam 0.95, one take).

| # | time | block | what it does | EP20 line |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Full EP20 script (voice text)
If you hide one hundred thousand dollars under your floor, so the IRS never sees it, you just built tax evasion. But cash never stays hidden. The moment you spend it, it leaves a trail. Buy a car with more than ten thousand in cash, and the dealer has to report it. Deposit it, and the bank has to report it too. Even your neighbor can report you online, without giving a name. Get caught, and you pay it all back, plus a seventy five percent fraud penalty, and up to five years in prison. So let's rewind, and build it legally. Put money in a 401(k), and up to twenty four thousand five hundred a year skips tax today. An HSA is tax free going in, tax free while it grows, and tax free for medical bills. In a Roth IRA, the growth is never taxed. Rent out your home for fourteen days or less a year, and that rent is tax free. Give each kid up to nineteen thousand dollars a year, with no gift tax. And watch the last one, because it has a twist. Live in your home two of the last five years, and up to two hundred fifty thousand dollars of profit is tax free when you sell. Five years. The same number as the prison sentence. Same money. One is a crime. The other is just reading the rules. So where's yours? Under the floor, in a 401(k), a Roth, or an HSA? Comment one.

## Style rules (what made it look good)
- HOOK look = GTA 6 cinematic: Vice City sunset (purple -> pink -> orange sky), palms, pastel house cutaway, black letterbox bars, GTA WANTED stars HUD filling 1->5 from the authority word to the crime word (flash red/blue at 5).
- Raid in the hook: black IRS SUVs + police skid in, agents in navy jackets with yellow IRS on the back run in and point; hero raises trembling hands; crime word = pure red text on a dark plate (MeshBasic, toneMapped off, no orange shift).
- Mute-clear: big green amount in frame 1, red crime word at the end of the hook; one-word captions; numbers as digits in captions ($10,000, 75%, 5 YEARS).
- One continuous camera; places change only with a whip to the sky/ceiling (dealer x 300, bank x -300, neighbour, IRS cell x 600, yard). Something new every ~2 s; characters always move (run, point, type, sit cuffed, cheer).
- Numbers colour code: GREEN legal money, RED crime/penalty, YELLOW key fact, WHITE neutral; story labels screen-anchored (SHOWS) so they never crop.
- Real props only: cash straps ($20 x 50 = $100,000), FORM 8300, BANK REPORT, laptop "REPORT TAX FRAUD", glass 401(k) vault, HSA cabinet, Roth money tree, 14 DAYS calendar, gift box, FOR SALE -> SOLD, prison cage.
- SFX: ting 0 s, money on the amount, realistic siren + tire screech + doors, whoosh on every whip, stamp/slap on reports, wrong on penalties, boom on prison and on the twist, tape-rewind, ting on green checks, pop on CTA options.
- Safety: consequences only, never "how to hide"; every number with an IRS source in facts.md; "up to"; eligibility note in the description.

## Same formula, new topics (ideas to verify first)
- "If you get paid cash under the table..." (caught: 1099/W-2 mismatch, bank, bosses audited -> legal: tips deduction, 401(k), standard deduction).
- "If you never file your taxes..." (failure-to-file 5%/month up to 25%, refund lost after 3 years -> legal: free filing, extension).
- "If you lie on your insurance claim..." (insurance fraud -> legal: deductible, bundling, claims rules).
- "If you hide crypto from the IRS..." (1099-DA from brokers -> legal: hold > 1 year = long-term rate, tax-loss harvesting).
