Keep sound effects sparse in Inside Money hooks: the user asked to "remove the many sound effects like pop and keep the words" (EP15 hook, 2026-11-06).

**Why:** a pop/ting/money cue on every beat cluttered the voice; the user wants the words clear.

**How to apply:** per 5 s hook use ~4 cues max: ting at 0 s (channel rule), whoosh only on big scene changes (map wipe, white flash), one cash sound on the key price. No pops on every icon. Related: [[inside-money-ep5-ep6-feedback-rules]].
