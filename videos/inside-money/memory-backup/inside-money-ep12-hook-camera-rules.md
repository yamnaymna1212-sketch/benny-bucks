User feedback 2026-11-04 (EP12 dirty money):
- Hook must be understood with no sound; only elements that serve the hook (no decor like shelves/bottles); key object big (washer) and the money clearly visible in the right place.
- Main figure must contrast with the background (black-clad criminal on a dark room was rejected) -> brighter backdrop + rim light.
- One continuous camera; the camera must NEVER enter/pass through any object (wall, door glass, car, person). Glass-door "wipes" were rejected. Put interiors inside real buildings with open doorways and route the camera around/above things.
- Check hands: characters' hands must not cross or go inside each other.

**Why:** the user felt the hook was confusing and the transitions looked like glitches.
**How to apply:** before every render take stills at every transition and look for frames filled by a near object; see [[inside-money-pacing-cta-rules]] and [[inside-money-hook-zoom-captions]].
