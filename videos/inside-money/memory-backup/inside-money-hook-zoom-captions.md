User rule (2026-11-04, EP11 hook v1 rejected: "too close, no house, no digging underground visible"):
- Second 1 = a MEDIUM-WIDE shot (not too far, not too close) where the viewer understands the whole topic with no sound: the place (e.g. the house), the process (e.g. a cross-section of the ground under the garden with the hole), the money, and a clear character ACTION (e.g. Mike digging, dirt flying).
- Then ZOOM in, then a tighter ZOOM on the one key element, and make that element glow while the rest goes darker (like the reference: a lit yellow cable in a dark room). Never distract: one focus per shot.
- Captions: ONE word at a time, never sentences. When a price/number is shown on screen as 3D text, remove it from the captions (no repetition).

**Why:** viewers must get the video's topic from the first frame; close-ups without context confuse them.
**How to apply:** every hook beat = wide (context) -> zoom -> tight glowing zoom (vignette up). Build captions with 1-word chunks and skip `$`/number words that float in 3D. Related: [[inside-money-glow-highlight-style]], [[inside-money-pacing-cta-rules]], [[inside-money-ep5-ep6-feedback-rules]].
