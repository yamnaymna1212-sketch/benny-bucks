Crude is priced on the world market, not in Washington. This year, shipping through the Strait of Hormuz was disrupted. Refining is about a quarter, distribution thirteen percent, taxes thirteen percent. A pause also needs the president's signature. And pump prices take weeks to follow crude. So the new Congress can touch one small slice. Will gas be lower next year? Nobody can promise that. Vote: UP or DOWN? Comment one word.
