## HOOK
1. What if you dug 200 meters under the bank that stores $800 billion in gold?
2. Here's what's down there. And what's at the bottom isn't gold.

## STAGE 1 - 80 ft / 24 m: the vault
3. At 80 feet, you hit the vault. Over six thousand tons of gold.
4. About half a million bars. And none of it belongs to the bank.
5. It's stored for foreign central banks and the U.S. Treasury.
6. One sealed room. Its only door is a ninety-ton steel cylinder.
7. It takes three people to open it.

## STAGE 2 - ~180 ft / 55 m: subway
9. Keep going. At over 170 feet, you pass the deepest subway station in New York.

## STAGE 3 - heat and water
10. Now it gets hot. Every 100 meters adds about 3 degrees Celsius.
11. At 200 meters, that's 5 to 6 degrees warmer than the surface.
12. And if water breaks in? The pressure hits twenty times the air around you.

## OPEN LOOP
13. Keep going. Something huge is right below you.

## REVEAL - ~500 ft / 152 m: water tunnel
14. At about 500 feet, you hit a tunnel twelve feet wide.
15. Water Tunnel Number 3.
16. It brings Manhattan 350 million gallons of water. Every single day.
17. No gold. This is what the city can't live without.
18. At 200 meters, you're already below it.
19. Down here? Just rock.

## CTA (vote)
20. So what's worth more under New York... the gold, or the water? Comment one.
