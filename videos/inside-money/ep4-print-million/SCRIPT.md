# EP4 — "Why can't the government print everyone a million?"  (DRAFT v1, 2026-10-31)
Hook is the user's line. Character: Mike (inside the cutaway). Target 61-65 s (~190-214 words) -> measure after voice.

1. Why can't the government just print a million dollars for every person and make everyone rich?   [HOOK: Mike at a giant printing press, counter "342,000,000 people"]
2. Let's do the math. America has about 342 million people.                                        [crowd of Mikes / population counter]
3. A million dollars each? That's 342 trillion dollars.                                             [digits stack up into a tower, Mike looks up]
4. All the money in America right now? About 23 trillion.                                           [small tower beside the huge one]
5. So we'd make almost 15 times more money than exists.                                             [x15 stamp, Mike's jaw drops]
6. But the stuff stays the same. Same houses. Same burgers. Same gas.                                [3 objects on a shelf, unchanged]
7. Now everyone has a million, and every seller knows it.                                           [store counter, customers waving cash]
8. The burger man raises his price. So does the landlord. So does the gas station.                  [price tags flip up one by one]
9. Prices climb, and your million buys far less than you thought.                                   [wallet shrinks / bill shrinks]
10. This isn't a theory. Zimbabwe printed so much that it issued a 100 trillion dollar note.        [giant note, Mike holds it]
11. Today the Federal Reserve orders about 4 to 5 billion new bills a year.                         [REVEAL ~75%: press floor, bills fly]
12. That's roughly 109 to 140 billion dollars. Not 342 trillion.                                    [small vs huge tower]
13. Money works because there's less of it than the things you can buy.                             [scale: bills vs objects]
14. If everyone gets a million, nobody actually gets richer.                                        [Mike shrugs]
15. Would you still take the million? Tell me below.                                                [question card + pinned comment]

Pinned comment: "Would you still take the million if everything doubled in price? Yes or no?"
