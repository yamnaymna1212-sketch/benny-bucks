7. Behind the glass the teller types a quiet note. Danny never sees it. Banks never tell you.
8. Three weeks later, a gray sedan stops outside his shop. A woman steps out. IRS. (SHOT: badge)
9. She opens a folder with every deposit. Then a second folder. Danny has never seen it.
10. Whose name is on it? CTA: Did the bank catch him? Comment YES or NO. Part two is coming.
