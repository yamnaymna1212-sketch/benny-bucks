# EP14 script v1 (draft, ~200 words; one line = one shot). Voice = user's ElevenLabs Zack; amounts written in words.
1. Trump presses the nuclear button on Iran. What happens to your wallet?
2. Honestly? Nobody knows. Nothing like it has happened since nineteen forty-five, so any number would be a guess.
3. But we do know what even a regular war with Iran does to oil.
4. Before the war this year, a barrel cost about seventy dollars.
5. In March, it hit about one hundred twenty-five.
6. Why? One narrow sea. The Strait of Hormuz. About one fifth of the world's oil sails through it.
7. Last June, one strike on Iran pushed oil up seven percent in a single day.
8. And stocks fell, while investors ran to gold.
9. Now your gas station. Oil moves, pumps follow.
10. In January, a gallon cost about two dollars eighty-two. This fall, about four twenty-seven.
11. That's roughly twenty-two dollars more every time you fill a fifteen gallon tank.
12. Trucks and ships burn that fuel too. So the price of things that travel goes up.
13. And when peace talks start, oil drops again. Fear is priced in days, not years.
14. So which hits your wallet first? Comment GAS or STOCKS.
