## Facts to verify before the render (sources found 2026-10-23)
- New-car depreciation: about 20% in year 1, about 60% after 5 years (Edmunds, Kelley Blue Book). Sources disagree: Carfax-based figures are lower (about 40% retained) and iSeeCars used data says about 42% after 5 years. The script says "about sixty percent" for new cars. Fine for a $250k car, but it is a rough average, not exact for any model.
- Loan: Experian Q2 2026 average new-car APR 6.35%, about 69 months. My math for $250,000 at 6.35% over 72 months: payment about $4,180 a month, interest about $51,000 total.
- $250,000 x 60% loss = $150,000.
- "Old car loses very little" = general rule (depreciation slows after year one), not a precise number.
- Tesla Roadster: it has been announced for years and has no confirmed price. The $250,000 number on the hook is the user's, so the voice never says "Tesla". The car in the image only shows it.
