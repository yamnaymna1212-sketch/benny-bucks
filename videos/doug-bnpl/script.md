# DOUG PAYS · EP. 1 — Buy Now, Pay Later (groceries)
Target 61–65 s, ~165 words. Hook card: ONE number ($20 now / $80 groceries). Sources on cards: LendingTree BNPL surveys (Apr 2025).

1. You pay $20 now for $80 of groceries. Sounds smart. It is not.
2. This is Doug. Doug is hungry.
3. Doug want salmon. Salmon cost 8 fish. Doug have 2 fish.
4. Fox say: Take salmon now. Pay 2 fish. Pay 2 fish. Pay 2 fish. Pay 2 fish.
5. Doug happy. Doug eat salmon.
6. Two weeks later. Salmon gone. Payments still here.
7. Doug hungry again. Doug buy more salmon. More payments.
8. Payments on payments on payments.
9. This is called buy now, pay later.
10. Doug poor.
11. In real life, about 1 in 4 buy-now-pay-later users bought groceries with it. One year before, 1 in 7.
12. And about 4 in 10 paid a bill late that year. Late fee. Doug cry.
13. Doug rule: If it is food, pay with fish you have. No fish? Buy less salmon.
14. Save this for payday.
15. Next time: Why Doug credit score fall? Did you ever pay groceries in 4 payments? Yes or no.

Facts to cite on screen: "~25% of BNPL users used it for groceries, up from ~14% a year earlier" (LendingTree, Apr 2025, 980 BNPL users); "41% paid a BNPL bill late in the past year, up from 34%" (LendingTree, Apr 2025 — base = respondents, say "about 4 in 10"; the 2026 report says 47% of users).
Reward for viewer = line 13 (rule) + line 14 (save). Gag = "Doug poor." (line 10).
