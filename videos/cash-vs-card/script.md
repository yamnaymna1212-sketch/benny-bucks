Voices: narrator `am_michael`, Benny `am_puck`, speed 1.18 (`tools/tts.py`, `lines.txt`). Hook question "who secretly paid extra?" is answered at the end (cash payers help fund card rewards).

## Fact check
| Claim | Basis |
|---|---|
| ~24¢ on a $10 card sale; avg 2.36% | Nilson 2025: Visa/Mastercard credit avg swipe fee 2.36% → $10 × 2.36% = 23.6¢ (CMSPI estimates higher, 2.91%) |
| Some shops add up to 3% | Visa surcharge cap 3% (credit only; debit surcharges barred; some states ban/cap) |
| MIT: card payers bid more than double | Prelec & Simester (MIT Sloan, 2001) Celtics/Red Sox ticket auction |
| ~22% a year if you carry a balance | Fed G.19: 22.15% (Q2 2026) – 22.36% (Aug 2026), accounts assessed interest |
| Cash payers help fund card rewards | Schuh, Shy & Stavins, Boston Fed PPDP 10-3 (2010) |

Sources: fool.com (Nilson) · cardrates.com · paytia.com / digitaltransactions.net (surcharge) · betterworld.mit.edu (MIT study) · bostonfed.org · walnutinvest.com / FRED TERMCBCCINTNS
